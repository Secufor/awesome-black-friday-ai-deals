## Add a deal

Two ways, both welcome, including for your own product:

- **No code:** open a [Submit a deal](../../issues/new?template=submit-deal.yml) issue and we'll add it.
- **Pull request:** add one object to [`data/deals.json`](data/deals.json), run `python3 scripts/build_readme.py`, and open a PR. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The rules are short: the deal must be live, stated on a page the vendor controls, and linked without affiliate or tracking parameters.

## How this list is kept honest

- Every row links to the vendor page where the deal was seen, and was checked on the date at the top.
- No affiliate links, no paid placements, no "up to 90% off" without a source.
- Expired deals are removed after Cyber Monday; next year's deals go into the same list.

Maintained by the team behind [GetYouTubeTranscript](https://getyoutubetranscript.com). Our own entry is in the list and labelled as ours; every other entry is unaffiliated.
